Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This tool is operated by a single creator for their own TikTok account. It does not serve a public user base.

What we collect

  • TikTok data you authorize: via TikTok LoginKit/OAuth we receive an access token, your TikTok user ID (open_id), and username/ avatar only if the user.info.basic scope is granted. These are used only to upload videos to your own draft inbox and to confirm posting status.
  • Video files: quiz videos generated locally are transferred to TikTok's servers for the upload flow. We do not retain copies on third-party servers beyond what the upload protocol requires.

What we do NOT do

  • No selling, sharing, or advertising of any data.
  • No analytics beyond success/failure logs of the upload itself.
  • OAuth tokens and app credentials are stored locally on the operator's own machine, never published.

Your rights

You can revoke access at any time via TikTok Settings > Account > Deactivate this device / Manage Apps, or by deleting the app authorization. Data processed through TikTok is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.
